# Date polls and game nights

Prefix: `DP`

A host asks a group which date suits them, optionally which games too, and the pick makes a game day or a game night.

## Starting a poll

- **DP-1** A date poll needs a title and two to fourteen distinct dates, none in the past (today is fine), in a real time zone; its dates are put in order and repeats dropped.
- **DP-2** The host is in their own poll from the start.
- **DP-3** The poll form can grow more date fields, up to the fourteen a poll takes.
- **DP-4** A refused poll form says why and keeps what was typed, the ticked games included.

## Joining

- **DP-5** A poll is only for the people who joined it through its invite link: to anyone else its page and actions don't exist, and a crafted link joins nobody.
- **DP-6** The invite shows the poll's title and dates but not its place; joining takes you to the poll, someone signed out joins after signing in or signing up, and everyone in the poll sees the link to share.
- **DP-7** A player joins a poll once; an unknown or picked poll can't be joined, and a picked poll's invite says the date is picked, except to someone already in it, who lands on the poll.

## Answering dates

- **DP-8** Everyone in the poll says which of its dates they can make and can change it until the pick; ticking none is an answer too.
- **DP-9** Only people in the poll answer, and only with the poll's own dates.
- **DP-10** People answer independently: one person's answer never blocks or counts as another's.
- **DP-11** A date that has passed can't be changed any more, but an answer already given for it is kept.

## Results

- **DP-12** The results show per date who can and who can't come, and mark as best the date most people can make, ties going to the earliest, none while nobody can.
- **DP-13** The host sees who is still to answer.
- **DP-14** The home page lists my open polls with how many have answered, and offers to start one; a picked poll drops off it.

## Picking a date

- **DP-15** Only the host picks a date, once, from the poll's options and not one that has passed.
- **DP-16** Picking a date in a poll without games creates the game day with the poll's title, place and time zone, the host, and everyone whose latest answer says they can make it.
- **DP-17** An answer or offer that arrives while the host is picking is taken into account, so nobody who said yes is left out.
- **DP-18** After the pick the poll takes no more joins or answers, marks the picked date and links to what it made; if that game day was cancelled, the poll says so.

## Games on a poll

- **DP-19** The host can choose to ask about games when starting a poll; a poll that doesn't refuses game changes and says why.
- **DP-20** Only the host proposes games, while the poll is open and at most 12, from their own collection on the poll form or by BGG search; the host never sees friends' collections.
- **DP-21** The host can take a game off the poll, and its yeses and offers to bring it go with it.
- **DP-22** Everyone in the poll says yes or no per game and can change it until the pick; a yes is a wish, at most 3 each, and a fourth is refused with the reason.
- **DP-23** Only people in the poll answer about games, and only about the poll's own games.
- **DP-24** Anyone in the poll can offer to bring a game, whether or not they own it, or say not this time; someone with a proposed game in their collection is asked once whether they'll bring it.
- **DP-25** Each game shows who wants to play it and who brings it, or that nobody does yet; each date shows which games that day would lose, and why.
- **DP-26** A game is brought by the first person who offered and can come; if they take it back, the next one who offered brings it.
- **DP-27** The games are signposted next to the dates, with how many are left to answer and how many yeses are left, and the host's add buttons lead to the search.
- **DP-28** Each button on a game card names the game it is about.

## Game nights

- **DP-29** Picking a date in a poll with games makes a game night instead of a game day, with the date, title, place, time zone, the host and everyone who can come, its plan (each game someone coming offered to bring, who brings it, who else offered and who coming wants it) and the games nobody coming can bring.
- **DP-30** A game night is only for the people on it; someone in the poll who wasn't available is told so.
- **DP-31** After the pick the poll links to the game night, and it shows on the home page.
- **DP-32** A game night's table gets suggestions from its plan, with the yeses as wishes.

## Erasure

- **DP-33** Someone erased before the pick isn't on the game day or game night it makes, and their yeses and offers go with them.
- **DP-34** An erased player leaves the game night, and a game they were to bring passes to the next one coming who offered, or goes missing.
- **DP-35** A poll whose host was erased is over: no joining, answering, offering or picking, its page says so, it leaves the home page, and its invite lets nobody in.
