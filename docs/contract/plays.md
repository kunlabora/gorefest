# Plays

Prefix: `PL`

The play log, the checkpoint question after a play, and telling what you watched.

## The play log

- **PL-1** A joined player logs what was played, who played it and roughly how long, for players who joined the game day.
- **PL-2** A log needs at least one player, only players of the game day and a real duration; a refused log keeps the form as filled in and says what's missing ("Tick who played").
- **PL-3** Plays can be logged from the game day's date, judged in its own time zone and following the date when it moves; before that a log is refused ("The game day hasn't started yet") and the hub doesn't offer it.
- **PL-4** After the game day ended, a forgotten play can still be logged; a cancelled game day takes no plays.
- **PL-5** A play never names someone who was erased, and a player without a name shows as "Someone" in what was played.
- **PL-6** The log form offers the shelf's games, has me ticked at the table and a duration picked, and a game that isn't on the shelf can be found by search, without repeating the shelf's games.
- **PL-7** The log page walks through three steps (the game, who played, how long) with a summary of what will be logged, also in the phone dock.
- **PL-8** On the day, the hub's checkpoint asks what you played once you have a taste, and otherwise offers the taste round first with the log one tap away; the "Played today" section keeps a link to the log.
- **PL-9** The game day hub lists what was played, with who played and for how long.
- **PL-10** A logged play marks the game played for everyone at the table.
- **PL-11** Logged plays teach how fast the group plays: the median ratio to the box time over plays with mostly the same table, kept within bounds, where games without a playtime don't count.

## The question after a play

- **PL-12** After logging, a player who was at the table gets one "which did you enjoy more?" comparison against a game they played that we know least about, then fresh suggestions for that table, or the hub once the game day has ended.
- **PL-13** With nothing to compare against, logging goes straight to suggestions; logging a table you weren't at asks you nothing and records nothing, even for a hand-made answer.
- **PL-14** The page after logging ignores players who aren't on the game day, junk and out-of-range minutes.

## Watched a game

- **PL-15** On the day, the hub offers "Watched a game?", which lists the whole shelf except what you played, with a search for any other game.
- **PL-16** A watched game is held against one you played ("Now that you've seen it, which would you rather play?"); the answer counts for taste but doesn't make the game played.
- **PL-17** "Can't tell yet" records nothing; with nothing you played to compare against (owning isn't playing) it says so; only a known game you haven't played, held against one you have, counts.
- **PL-18** Once the game day has ended, nothing watched can be told any more.
