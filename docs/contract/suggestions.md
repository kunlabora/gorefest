# Suggestions

Prefix: `SU`

Which games a table is offered, and why.

## Candidates

- **SU-1** Only shelf games that suit the number of players at the table and fit the time budget are suggested.
- **SU-2** A game's expected length follows the player count across BGG's range, rounded to 5 minutes, and scales with the group's own pace.
- **SU-3** A game without a playing time counts as an hour, never as quick.
- **SU-4** A game with a wild playing time range (a campaign game: max over 8 times the min and over 240 minutes) counts as its typical time for any table size, in the estimate and the time budget alike; when BGG's typical time is missing or just repeats the max, the geometric mean of the range stands in.
- **SU-5** An honest wide range is not wild, and keeps scaling with the player count.

## Ranking

- **SU-6** A table gets its top 3 candidates, each with a one-line reason.
- **SU-7** One player who is likely to be miserable sinks a game that the others love.
- **SU-8** Games nobody at the table has played get a novelty bonus, and the reason says so.
- **SU-9** When everyone who played a game rated it highly, it is a safe bet, and the reason says how many of them.
- **SU-10** Each player at the table who wishes to play a game adds a bonus, for at most 3 people, and the reason names who wants it; wishes of people not at the table don't count.
- **SU-11** An owner at the table who brought a game and is the one wishing to play it gets it suggested, with a reason naming them.
- **SU-12** A game a player said they haven't played is neutral for them, with no guess from what it's like: it is scored on the others and the novelty bonus, and the reason names who it is new to, unless someone wants to play it.
- **SU-13** A game a player watched but never played gets an enjoyment guess from what they saw and still counts as new.
- **SU-14** Players with fewer than 10 answers count as unknown; when nobody at the table is known, the reasons only talk about fit, never about enjoyment.
- **SU-15** Every reason and every "nothing fits" case has its own sentence, with the wording pinned.

## The table page

- **SU-16** The game day offers a way to pick a game for a table.
- **SU-17** Everyone who joined starts ticked at the table with any length, and suggestions show right away; ticking players or a length asks for new suggestions in place, and people who aren't on the game day are ignored.
- **SU-18** The top pick stands alone and the other two are compact; a bar says how many are at the table and how long they have, and each pick says it's "for you" at a table of one or "for the N of you".
- **SU-19** Each suggestion says what kind of game it is.
- **SU-20** Unticking everybody means nobody is playing, and the page asks who is.
- **SU-21** When nothing plays well with that many players, the page says so.
- **SU-22** When nothing fits the time, the page says so and offers one tap to the shortest length that fits, with how many games fit then.
- **SU-23** When nobody at the table has a known taste, the page says the picks go by player count and playing time and offers the taste round; when only others are unknown, it names them and leaves the viewer's own round out; a player who finished a round is never called unknown.
