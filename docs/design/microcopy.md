# Microcopy

Sample copy in the house voice. Keep the structure; change the words freely.

## Name

"Gorefest" is our group's name for its game days. Candidates for the product:

| Name | Why it fits | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup Phase** | The app lives in the bit before the game starts, the part it's meant to make shorter. Gamers know the term. | Two words; the logo gets long. |
| **First Player** | The token that ends the dithering and starts the game. Short and confident. | Common phrase, so it's harder to search for. |
| **Shelfie** | Built on our own "shelf" word, and playful. | Other apps already use it. |

## Wizard

- Question, unplayed pair: **Which would you rather try?** (tag: "Unplayed pair")
- Question, played pair: **Which did you enjoy more?** (tag: "You've played both")
- Answers: tap a game · **Haven't played either** · **Both meh** · **Both great**, plus **Haven't played this** under each game. "Haven't played" only ever means that: never "Don't know", which could also mean "I can't choose".
- Stop early: **Done for now**
- Returning top-up: "Two new games on the shelf. Four quick pairs will sharpen your picks."

## Payoff

- Kicker: "Round done · 12 answers"
- Heading: **Your likely favourites today**
- Reasons: "Big engine, lots of cards: right in your wheelhouse." · "You loved heavy economic games so far." · "Short and calm, for when your brain needs a break."
- Taste twin (optional): "**You and Bram** both lean towards heavy euros. Grab a table together."
- Empty shelf: **Nice taste. Now we need games.**
- Actions: "Open Gorefest #7" · "Sharpen it: 5 more pairs"

## Suggestion reasons

One line, about 60 characters, naming people where it helps:

- Novelty: "Nobody's played it and everyone's likely to enjoy it."
- New to someone (they said they haven't played it): "New to Kim and Anna: a chance to try it." Never claims they'll enjoy it.
- Owner's wish: "Kim brought it and has been itching to play."
- Wished by the table: "Kim really wants to play this." · "Kim and Anna both want to play this." · "Three of you really want to play this."
- Consensus: "Safe bet: all four of you rated it highly."
- Time: "Fits your 90 minutes with room for snacks."
- Player count: "Best at exactly five, which is you."
- Nothing fits: **Nothing fits 7 players in 45 minutes** → "The shelf's short on big-group games. Loosen one thing:" [Try 90 min: 3 games fit]

## Checkpoint

- Kicker: "Lunch checkpoint" / "Evening checkpoint"
- **What did you play?** · **Who played?** · **Roughly how long?**
- Confirmation: "Logged Cascadia: 3 players, 45 min."
- Follow-up: "One quick question" → **Which did you enjoy more?**

## Invite and login

- "Anna invites you to a game day" · **Join Gorefest #7** · "Just your email. No password, no app to install."
- **What's your email?** · "We'll send a link and a 6-digit code. No password to remember." · [Send me a code]
- **Check your mail** · "Opened this from WhatsApp or Signal? The code is the quicker route." · [Let me in]
- Wrong code: "That code doesn't match. Two more tries, then we'll send a fresh one."
- Mail subject: "Your Gorefest code: 391740". The code goes in the subject so it shows in the notification.

## Shelf wishes

- Section on the bring page: **What do you want to play?** · "Heart up to 3 games on the shelf you're really keen on. Each heart nudges a game up the suggestions."
- Toggle: **I want to play this** / **I really want to play this** · count: "2 people want this"
- Fourth heart: "You can pick 3 favourites: take one off first"
- Empty: "Nothing on the shelf yet. Tick what you're bringing above."

## Bring requests

- Ask screen: kicker **Ask someone** · **Who could bring what?** · "Fancy a game you know somebody owns? Ask them to bring it. They answer with one tap, and you can ask up to 3." · buttons **Ask** / **See games** / **Take it back**
- States: **Asked** · **Bringing it** · "Anna is bringing it" · a decline reads only **Not this time**, no reason and no blame.
- Owner's section on the bring page: **People are asking** · "Bram and Caro ask if you can bring Brass: Birmingham" · **Bring it** / **Not this time**. Never "Rejected" or "Declined".
- Hub next step: kicker **Waiting for you** · "Can you bring Brass: Birmingham?" · "Bram asked you to bring it. One tap to say yes, or not this time." · **See what's asked**. Home tag: **Asked to bring a game**.
- Switch on the collection page: **Let people see my collection to ask for games** · "Only people on a game day you joined see your collection, and only to ask you for a game. You always get to say "Not this time"."
- Rejections: "You've asked for 3 games already: take one back first" · "They've been asked a lot already: leave it to them" · "They'd rather not be asked to bring games" · "They're already bringing that one"

## Game type and time

- Under a game's name: up to two words from BGG, uppercase small: "ABSTRACT · TILE PLACING", "COOPERATIVE", "CARD GAME".
- Campaign games with a silly BGG range show the typical time: "~120 min", not "5–1000 min".
