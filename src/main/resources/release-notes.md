## 2.5.0 · 2026-11-17

### New

- Mark the games you can explain. Suggestions lean towards a game someone at the table can teach, and say who.
- A play logged by mistake can be removed, and logged again the right way.
- The version in the footer opens What's new: what's new, changed and fixed in every version.

### Changed

- Linking your BGG account is just typing your username, without a code in your BGG profile. A household can share one account, and each person keeps their own collection.
- Logging a play starts with your last table, and a suggestion logs the table it was made for.
- Too few players for a game is a warning, not a refusal, so a play with someone who has no account still logs. A table of one asks "Just you?" first.

### Fixed

- Double-clicking "Create game day" or "Log it" no longer makes two.

## 2.4.1 · 2026-11-13

### Fixed

- Login codes and links now work for an hour instead of 15 minutes, so a slow mail still arrives in time. The same goes for the code that confirms a new email address.

## 2.4.0 · 2026-11-12

### Changed

- New accounts are in at once, without waiting for approval. The admin hears about every sign-up and can still turn away someone nobody knows.
- Screen readers hear every page's headings in order, and are told what changed after each tap.
- Contrast, focus outlines and button sizes meet the accessibility guidelines in light and dark, and animations stay still if your device asks for less motion.
- The ask-to-bring screen has more room.

## 2.3.0 · 2026-10-30

### Changed

- Every form field, button, link and image tells a screen reader what it is.
- A group of game tiles is read out with the question it answers.

## 2.2.1 · 2026-10-29

### Fixed

- A pasted login code keeps all six digits.
- A forgotten play can still be logged after the game day.

## 2.2.0 · 2026-10-15

### New

- A date poll can also ask about games: the host proposes a few, people say which they'd play and offer to bring them, and the pick makes a game night.
- Watched a table play a game? Saying which you liked better now counts towards your taste.

### Changed

- The collection switch says what it covers: "Let people see my collection to ask for games".

### Fixed

- Long titles wrap, and poll wording, limits and labels are clearer.
- After watching a game, you only compare it with games you have played yourself.

## 2.1.1 · 2026-10-14

### Fixed

- The account and privacy pages look right straight after an update.
- "Which did you enjoy more?" is only asked about plays you were in.
- A deleted account's games, wishes and requests leave game days still to come.

## 2.1.0 · 2026-10-12

### New

- A privacy notice, linked from every page's footer.
- Your account page: change your email address, unlink BGG, ask for your data or have your account deleted.
- Ask someone on the game day to bring a game from their collection, or switch that off for yours.

### Changed

- The app is called Boardfest now.
- Ended and cancelled game days move to a "Past" section on the home page.
- After linking BGG, you carry on where you were going.
- The invite page and its preview no longer show where the game day is; you see it once you've joined.

### Fixed

- A reloaded taste round carries on where it was.
- A second tap on a form that's already sending does nothing.
- A search that finds nothing says so.
- Long game names wrap between words.
- Each "Haven't played" button names its game.

## 2.0.0 · 2026-10-08

### Changed

- Every screen redrawn, on phone and desktop: the home page, game day, bringing games, the taste round, suggestions and logging a play.

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-06

### New

- The first Boardfest: game days with invite links, bringing games from your BGG collection, the taste round, and suggestions for your table.
- Heart up to three games on the shelf you really want to play.
- Join straight after signing up from an invite, and cancel a game day you host.
- Log what you played during the game day.
